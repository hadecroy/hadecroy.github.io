AI-Driven Structural Optimisation of Cantilever Plates

Role: Applied AI Engineer • Bristol, UK • Nov 2025 – Dec 2025

TensorFlowSurrogateModelSLSQPYieldConstraintFEAData

Objective / challenge

  • Minimise mass under combined loading while respecting yield constraints.
  • Reduce compute cost by learning a surrogate model from FEA-derived data.

My approach

  • Trained a DNN to predict stress fields from thickness inputs (TensorFlow).
  • Achieved R² = 0.92/0.86 (train/validation).
  • Optimised thickness using SLSQP under yield constraints.

Results + validation

  • Compliant ~40% mass reduction under yield constraints.
  • Model accuracy quantified via R² on train/validation splits.
  • Proposed digital twin concept with sensor data fusion for predictive maintenance (concept-level).

What I’d improve next

  • Calibrate surrogate uncertainty (e.g., ensembles) to avoid “confidently wrong” predictions (TODO if you extend).
  • Add verification plots and constraint margins as standard deliverables in the case study visuals.

Diffractive Solar Sailing Research Placement

Role: Spaceflight Dynamics, Navigation & Control Engineer • Glasgow, UK • Oct 2023 – Apr 2024

MATLABDynamicsAttitudeTradeStudy

Objective / challenge

  • Model diffractive sail dynamics to support heliocentric trajectories (inward spiralling + inclination cranking).
  • Compare performance versus reflective sails under a heuristic force law roll manoeuvre strategy.

My approach

  • Implemented MATLAB simulation framework for diffractive sail dynamics.
  • Ran attitude (Euler angle) and diffraction angle trade studies.
  • Quantified trajectory outcomes for performance comparison.

Results + validation

  • Validated diffractive sails reached target orbit 20.4% faster than reflective sails.
  • Explored diffraction angle trade-offs (e.g., 112.5°: slightly better radial performance, reduced inclination performance).

What I’d improve next

  • Add clearer “assumptions & limitations” panel to the public case study (to make scope explicit).
  • Package key plots (time-to-orbit, inclination change, sensitivity) as a 1-page appendix visual.
